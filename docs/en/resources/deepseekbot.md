## About

DeepSeekBot: the open-source GrokBot alternative, built on DeepSeek Harness (DSH). PersonaBots with their own identity, persona and Git-backed memory you can share via GitHub; group chat, task delegation, and IM identities on Lark/Feishu, Slack, Discord and WeChat. Works with other DSH plugins. Bot marketplace: market.botharness.ai. MIT.
